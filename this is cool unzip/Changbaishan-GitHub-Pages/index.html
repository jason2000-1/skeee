INTERNATIONAL ARRIVALS

Before leaving home

  • Passport & hotel registration. Bring the original travel document. Hotels register foreign guests; private accommodation requires a separate registration arrangement. NIA guidance.
  • Payments. Set up a supported Alipay or WeChat Pay payment method before travelling and carry a backup card and some cash. Acceptance varies. Visitor payment guide.
  • Stay connected. Arrange roaming or a suitable SIM. Some overseas websites are inaccessible in mainland China; save hotel addresses, tickets and useful translations offline.
TRAVELLING WITHIN CHINA · 国内出行

Ready for the transfer

  • Original ID, matching booking. Bring the valid document used for tickets and hotel bookings. Mainland residents generally use their resident ID; international visitors should check passport verification requirements. 12306 ticket guidance.
  • Check the station name. The plan uses Changbaishan West / 长白山西站 for Wanda. Match the actual service and resort pickup; north-side attractions are a different road trip.
  • Domestic-flight power banks. CAAC prohibits power banks without a clear 3C mark, or of recalled models/batches, on mainland domestic flights. Carry-on only; capacity and airline restrictions also apply. This affects all passengers. 3C notice · Baggage rules.
  • Book the actual winter service. Confirm train tickets, resort shuttle times, attraction reservations and the return airport transfer. A previous season’s timetable is only a starting point.
FOR EVERYONE

Cold-weather essentials

  • Check snow-sports insurance. Standard cover may exclude skiing. Confirm the skiing, medical care and rescue cover needed for the planned activities. Insurance guidance.
  • Pack for warm-up breaks. Layers, gloves, warm footwear, spare socks and swimwear for any pool plans. Keep phone batteries warm and allow weather buffers.
  • Keep key details offline. Hotel names in Chinese, booking references, emergency contacts and the flight home. All itinerary times are Beijing time (UTC+8).
